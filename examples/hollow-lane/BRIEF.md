# Hollow Lane: brief

Hollow Lane is an invented business, made to test Fastidious end to end in a trade unlike the exemplars. Every fact below is invented for the test, and the finished page says so.

## Frame

A small Somerset cider maker sells out every year to people who already know it. It wants new customers who value patience over price, without becoming a brand that shouts. The problem is not reach: it is making slowness read as the point.

## Positioning

- **Person:** someone who buys good wine and is tired of sweet cider; they will wait for something made properly.
- **Pain:** cider is sold as cheap and sweet; the good stuff is hard to find and harder to trust.
- **Promise:** eleven months in oak from fourteen old trees on one lane, and a place on the spring list.

## Sign system (summary)

- **Myth:** patience is a craft. The long way round is chosen, not endured.
- **Signature signs:** the lane itself (a route that bends on purpose), a chalk tally on a barrel (the only clock), one russet apple carried from tree to glass.
- **World:** a hand-made tabletop diorama, the way a museum or a model railway shows a place it loves.
