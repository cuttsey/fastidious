## Verdicts

- **S01 and S06, light flags:** false positives. By eye every shadow falls to the right, so the sun is from the left as the bible says; the left of these frames is the dark studio, which makes the right side brighter. Kept. The brighter-side reading is a rough proxy, as the skill warns.
- **All six scenes: pick.** One world, one light, one palette; no people, text or brands in frame.
- **Story test:** with the copy hidden, the contact sheet reads orchard, apple, press, barrels, bottle and apple, orchard again. The close answers the opening.

## Still open

- The palette check with thirteen colours at a tolerance of 18 is permissive. For a stricter series, cut the palette to the five roles that matter and lower the tolerance.
- The felt canopies are smooth spheres; a real diorama would show more fibre. A future pass could add a fibre texture.
