# Codrops Creative Hub survey

Source: [Codrops Creative Hub, all demos made at Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/hub/all/codrops/), read 22 November 2026: 15 pages, **555 demos**, each with a GitHub repository. 363 are in the `codrops` organisation (MIT unless a repository says otherwise); 192 are by guest authors in their own repositories (licence read per repository); about 150 are WebGL, WebGPU, React Three Fiber or shader work; 29 are small "Sketch" studies.

## How the hub is worked through

Newest first, in intake runs of about ten. Each candidate goes through the five gates in `README.md`; resemblance to other sites is never a reason to reject. WebGL and WebGPU candidates are batched for a real-GPU review, because the headless check uses software rendering and is unfair to 3D. jQuery-era demos (roughly the oldest 150) are read for ideas, not code.
