# Primary's client work: what fit looks like

Primary is a brand studio that sells a two-week "Brand Sprint" to startups: two brand options, a senior team and one project at a time, for a flat fee ([pricing](https://www.primary.studio/pricing)). Its fourteen case studies ([work](https://www.primary.studio/work)) were observed in a browser on 13 November 2026. Each case's images (four to ten) were set into contact sheets, and their colours were measured by reducing each image to 96 pixels wide and averaging the most frequent colours. Hexes for Alan and Gist were read from the cases' own specimen boards. The studio's own site is a separate entry in `detail-craft/references/exemplars.md`.

Take the principles, never the specifics. Nothing here is a palette or a mark to copy.

## Fit: stakes set the register

Where customers risk money, health or reputation, the identities are restrained: Heron, Frame, Alan and Incentiv all use a hued deep ground, a warm off-white and one warm signal, with an editorial serif or a calm geometric sans. Where customers make things, the identities are loud and tactile: Lovable's gradient, Craft's paper and sky, Paper's collage, Weaver's checks, Gist's neon kit. Small owners and service teams get warmth: Owner's one green and star, Solidroad's sunlit road and roadrunner.

The register sets the loudness, but every strong case still has one surprise against its register. Alan's orange warms a clinical category. Heron's ochre and editorial birds make private credit feel cultured. Lovable keeps a calm cream ground under its gradient.

## Colour: five structures

| Structure | Cases | How it is built |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep ground, warm light, one signal | Alan, Heron, Incentiv, Frame | A navy, teal-navy or green-black; an off-white with a warm cast; one saturated warm note used for one job |
| One hue owns everything | Owner | One saturated colour on product, merch, stage and screens, with black |
| Gradient as identity | Lovable | A gradient that means something (a heartbeat), used as an object on a quiet ground |
| Colour kit | Gist, Weaver, Craft, Loti | Three or more distinct colours, named and used flat, so the system can be remixed |
| Picture palette | Solidroad, Paper | Colours taken from an illustrated or photographed world |

Three rules hold across the strong cases. Lights are tinted (none uses pure white as its main light). Darks carry hue (only Titan and Stellar use neutral near-black, and they are the least distinctive). The signal is rare and usually warm, and it does one job. `colour-director`'s `palette.py fit` checks all three, plus the register and ownership against competitors' colours.

## Type and material

- Editorial serifs for money: Martina on Heron and Frame, with IBM Plex Mono on Frame for figures.
- A humanist geometric sans for health: Centra on Alan, four weights, set large and loose.
- A family that stretches for creators: Instrument Serif, Sans, Semi Condensed and Condensed on Gist; Matter with Sequel Sans on Paper.
- Material is the idea made physical, and it shows first on merchandise: Weaver's knit patch, Lovable's socks, Paper's embossed cap, Owner's hoodie, Frame's leather and card.

## Approach

1. Name to idea: love to a heartbeat, paper to two sheets, weaver to crossing lines.
2. Idea to mark: one shape that works as a favicon.
3. Mark to motif: Owner's star, Weaver's crossings, Paper's sheets repeat as devices.
4. Motif to system: palette, type and material carry the motif at the register's loudness.
5. System to proof: billboards, subway posters, stages, merchandise, packaging and product screens.

Two directions in two weeks forces early commitment, so only ideas that already belong to the client survive.

## Where it is weaker

- Stellar: near-black with a purple glow and a literal star. The category's default, with nowhere to go.
- Titan: contrarian for security, but blue-to-orange chrome on black is a familiar retro code outside it.
- Incentiv: navy and teal is enterprise finance's default; the orange dot does the work.
- Device mock-ups recur in nearly every case. They prove the system works on screens but say little about the brand. The objects only that brand would make are the stronger proof.

The weaker cases are the ones where the category's code wins over the business's own idea. Their overviews also read as generic agency copy ("revolutionize", "cutting-edge"), while the stronger cases state one idea in one sentence.
